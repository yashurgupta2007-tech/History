Traditional Dances of Maharashtra

The folk dances of Maharashtra are an important part of the state's cultural heritage. They express the traditions, beliefs, celebrations and stories of its people.

From the energetic movements of the Lavani to the heroic storytelling of Powada, each dance form has its own unique style and cultural significance.

Lavani

Lavani is a vibrant traditional dance form of Maharashtra, known for its energetic movements, rhythmic music and expressive storytelling.

Learn More

Tamasha

Tamasha is a lively folk theatre tradition that combines music, dance, drama and storytelling.

Learn More

Koli Dance

Koli Dance reflects the life and traditions of Maharashtra's coastal fishing community.

Lezim

Lezim is a rhythmic group performance featuring the traditional Lezim instrument and energetic coordinated movements.

Learn More

Fugdi

Fugdi is a traditional group dance performed especially during festivals and celebrations.

Learn More

Gondhal

Gondhal is a traditional ritual performance associated with devotion and religious ceremonies.

Learn More

Powada

Powada is a heroic ballad tradition that celebrates the bravery and achievements of historical heroes.

Learn More

Dhangari Gaja

Dhangari Gaja is a traditional dance and folk performance associated with the Dhangar shepherd community of Maharashtra.

Learn more

Vasudeva

Vasudev is a traditional folk dance and itinerant community tradition from Maharashtra, India, where performers act as living incarnations of Lord Krishna.
