Maharashtrian Cuisine

Explore the rich flavours of Maharashtra through its famous traditional dishes, snacks, sweets and beverages.

Flavours of Maharashtra

Maharashtrian Cuisine is known for its diverse flavours, regional specialties and traditional recipes. From spicy street food to festive sweets, every dish reflects the culture and heritage of the state.

Vada Pav

Mumbai's iconic street food consisting of a spicy potato fritter served inside a soft bun with chutneys.

Learn More

Misal Pav

A spicy curry made with sprouted beans, topped with farsan, onions and served with fresh pav.

Learn More

Puran Poli

A festive sweet flatbread stuffed with jaggery and lentils, enjoyed during celebrations and festivals.

Learn More

Ukadiche Modak

Steamed rice flour dumplings filled with coconut and jaggery, especially prepared during Ganesh Chaturthi.

Learn More

Thalipeeth

A nutritious multigrain flatbread served with butter, curd or pickle.

Learn More

Pitla Bhakari

A classic rural meal of gram flour curry served with traditional Jowar or Bajra Bhakri.

Learn More

Alu Wadi

A popular snack made by rolling colocasia leaves with a spiced gram flour taste, steaming and frying them.

Learn More

Tambda Rassa

A fiery red Kolhapuri Mutton curry famous for its rich spices and bold flavour.

Learn More

Pandhra Rassa

A creamy white mutton broth from Kolhapur, traditionally served alongside Tambda Rassa.

Solkadhi

A refreshing drink made from coconut milk and Kokum, popular in the Konkan region.

Sabudana Khichdi

A light and flavourful dish prepared with tapioca pearls, peanuts and potatoes, often eaten during fasting.
