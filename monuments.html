Architectural Heritage of Maharashtra

Maharashtra is home to magnificent palaces, historic monuments and colonial-era buildings that showcase remarkable architecture, royal history and cultural significance. These landmarks preserve the legacy of different dynasties and historical periods.

Shaniwar Wada

An 18th century fortification in Pune served as the seat of the Peshwas of the Maratha Empire.

Aga Khan Palace

A historic palace in Pune known for its association with Mahatma Gandhi during India's freedom movement.

Bibi Ka Maqbara

A beautiful Mughal-era mausoleum in Aurangabad, often called as 'Taj of the Deccan'.

Gateway Of India

Mumbai's iconic waterfront monument built in 1924 to commemorate the visit of King George V and Queen Mary.

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT)

A UNESCO World Heritage railway station renowned for its magnificent Victorian Gothic architecture.

Rajabai Clock Tower

A landmark clock tower located within the University of Mumbai campus, famous for its Gothic Revival style.

Vishrambaug Wada

A grand Maratha mansion in Pune showcasing traditional wooden architecture and Peshwa craftsmanship.

Lal Mahal

A historic palace in Pune closely associated with the childhood of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.

Learn More

David Sassoon Library

One of Mumbai's finest heritage libraries, admired for its Victorian architecture and historic significance.
