Festivals That Bring Maharashtra Together

Maharashtra celebrates a wide variety of festivals that reflect its traditions, beliefs and community life. From grand public celebrations to traditional village festivals, these occasions bring people together through rituals, music, food and joy.

Ganesh Chaturthi

Grand celebrations dedicated to Lord Ganesha, featuring decorated idols, processions, music, and community gatherings.

Learn More

Gudi Padwa

The traditional Maharashtrian New Year, celebrated with Gudis, rangoli, traditional food, and festive rituals.

Learn More

Makar Sankranti

A harvest festival celebrated with tilgul sweets, kite flying, and the greeting “Tilgul Ghya, God God Bola.”

Learn More

Dahi Handi

A lively celebration of Krishna's childhood, where teams form human pyramids to break a suspended pot.

Learn More

Narali Purnima

A coastal festival where fishing communities offer coconuts to the sea and pray for safety and prosperity.

Learn More

Bail Pola

A traditional farmers' festival celebrating bulls, which are decorated and honored for their contribution to agriculture.
