Traditional Dress of Maharashtrian Men

Maharashtrian Men's attire reflects simplicity, dignity and cultural pride. Traditional garments and accessories are commonly worn during festivals, weddings and important religious occasions.

Dhoti

Dhoti is a traditional, unstitched lower garment for men in Maharashtra made from a rectangular piece of cotton or silk, typically wrapped around waist and legs.

Learn More

Kurta

A men's kurta is a traditional South Asian upper garment, featuring popular styles like straight-cut, short and Pathani.

Learn More

Pheta

Pheta is a traditional Maratha turban symbolizing honor, pride and valor which is commonly worn during weddings, festivals and formal welcoming ceremonies.

Learn More

Puneri Pagdi

The Puneri Pagdi is a distinctive ceremonial headgear from Pune, symbolizing dignity and cultural identity.

Learn More

Kolhapuri Chappals

Handmade leather sandals from Kolhapur, famous for their durability, craftsmanship and timeless design.

Learn More

Angarkha

Angarkha is a traditional long upper garment worn by Maharashtrian men, especially during royal ceremonies, cultural events and festive occasions.
