Historical Timeline of Maharashtra

Journey through the centuries and explore the events, dynasties and empires that shaped Maharashtra.

Prehistoric Maharashtra

Before 230 BCE

Evidence of Stone Age Settlements and early human communities has been found in various parts of Maharashtra.

Satavahana Dynasty

230 BCE - 220 CE

The first major dynasty of the Deccan known for trade, administration and patronage of Buddhism.

Learn More

Vakataka Dynasty

250 CE - 500 CE

The Vakatakas encouraged art and culture and the famous Ajanta Caves flourished during this period.

Chalukya Dyansty

6th Century - 8th Century

Strengthened administration and temple architecture across the Deccan.

Rashtrakuta Dyansty

12th Century - 14th Century

14th Century - 16th century

The Deccan Sultanates shaped Maharashtra's architecture, culture and administration.

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj founded the Maratha Empire and established Swarajya.
