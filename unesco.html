Global Heritage Of Maharashtra

Maharashtra is home to several UNESCO-recognized heritage sites that showcase exceptional artistic, architectural and cultural achievements. These landmarks attract visitors from across the world and preserve the state's rich legacy for future generations.

Ajanta Caves

Inscribed in 1983, Ajanta Caves are renowned for their ancient Buddhist paintings, sculptures and rock-cut monasteries dating back to 2nd Century BCE.

Learn More

Ellora Caves

A UNESCO World Heritage Site featuring Buddhist, Hindu and Jain cave temples, including the magnificent Kailasa Temple.

Learn More

Elephanta Caves

Located on Elephanta Island near Mumbai, these caves are famous for their impressive rock-cut sculptures dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Learn More

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus

Inscribed in 2004, the iconic railway station is a masterpiece of Victorian Gothic revival Architecture blended with traditional Indian influences.

Learn More

Victorian Gothic and Art Deco Ensembles

Mumbai's remarkable collection of Victorian Gothic and Art Deco buildings was recognized by UNESCO in 2018 for its outstanding urban architecture.

Learn More

Western Ghats

Recognized in 2012, the Western Ghats are one of the world's eight biodiversity hotspots, home to unique flora and fauna across Maharashtra.
