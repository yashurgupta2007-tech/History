The Land of Heritage and Valor

Maharashtra is a land of magnificent forts, ancient caves, powerful dynasties, rich traditions and inspiring leaders. From Satvahanas to the Maratha empire, the state's history reflects courage, culture and architectural brilliance that continues to shape India's heritage.

Journey Through Time

Explore the major eras that shaped Maharashtra's glorious heritage

Ancient Era

Satavahana Dynasty, rock-cut caves and early trade centers.

Medieval Era

Yadavas, Bahmani Sultanate and the Deccan kingdoms.

Maratha Empire

The rise of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and Swarajya.

Modern Era

Freedom movement and the formation of Maharashtra State.

Majestic Forts of Maharashtra

Discover the magnificent forts that symbolize the bravery, strategy and architectural brilliance of the Marathas.

Raigad Fort

The capital of the Maratha Empire and the coronation site of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.

Sinhagad Fort

Known for the heroic battle of Tanaji Malusare and its strategic location.

Pratapgad Fort

Famous for the historic battle between Shivaji Maharaj and Afzal Khan.

Culture and Traditions of Maharashtra

Explore the rich traditions, art forms, festivals and cultural heritage that makes Maharashtra unique.

Folk Dances

Lavani, Koli Dance, Dhangari Gaja, Lezim, etc. reflect Maharashtra's vibrant traditions.

Folk Songs

Powada, Bharud, Bhavageet, etc. preserve the state's historical and spiritual heritage.

Traditional Attire

Paithani sarees, Nauvari sarees, Pheta etc. represent Maharashtra's identity.

Cuisine

Enjoy traditional dishes like PuranPoli, MisalPav, Modak, Vada Pav, etc.

World Heritage and Historical Sites

Maharashtra is home to magnificent caves, temples and monuments that reflects its rich history and architecture.

Ajanta Caves

UNESCO World Heritage Sites famous for Buddhist paintings and sculptures.

Ellora Caves

Renowned for rock-cut temples representing Buddhism, Hinduism and Jainism.

Elephanta Caves

Known for magnificent sculptures dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Gateway Of India

An iconic monument symbolizing Mumbai's historical significance.
