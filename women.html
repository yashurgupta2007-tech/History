Elegance of Maharashtrian Women

Traditional Attire of Maharashtrian women represents grace, heritage and regional identity. Every garment and ornament carries cultural significance and is proudly worn during festivals, weddings and religious ceremonies.

Nauvari Saree

A traditional nine-yard saree draped in a unique style, symbolizing the grace, strength and heritage of Maharrashtrian women.

Paithani Saree

A luxurious silk saree woven in Paithan, famous for its colourful borders and beautiful peacock motifs.

A traditional Maharashtrian nose ring worn during weddings, festivals and important occasions.
