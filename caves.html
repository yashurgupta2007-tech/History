Ancient Wonders Carved in Stone

Maharashtra is home to some of the world's finest rock-cut cave complexes. These caves reflects centuries of artistic excellence, religious devotion and architectural brilliance, attracting visitors from across the globe.

Ajanta Caves

A UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for its ancient Buddhist paintings, sculptures and rock-cut monasteries dating from the 2nd Century BCE.

Learn More

Ellora Caves

A UNESCO World Heritage Site featuring magnificent Buddhist, Hindu and Jain cave temples, including the world-famous Kailasa temple.

Learn More

Elephanta Caves

Located on Elephanta Island near Mumbai, these caves are renowned for their impressive rock-cut sculptures dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Learn More

Karla Caves

Home to one of India's largest and best-preserved Buddhist Chaitya halls, showcasing remarkable ancient architecture.

Learn More

Bhaja Caves

A group of ancient Buddhist Caves known for their beautifully carved prayer halls and monasteries near Lonavala.

Learn More

Kanheri Caves

Situated within Sanjay Gandhi National Park, these caves served as an important Buddhist learning and meditation centre.

Learn More

Pandavleni Caves

Ancient Buddhist Caves near Nashik featuring inscriptions, prayer halls and monastic residences carved into the hills.

Learn More

Bedse Caves

Peaceful Buddhist Caves admired for their elegant pillars, detailed carvings and scenic surroundings near Pune.

Learn More

Jogeshwari Caves

Among Mumbai's oldest rock-cut cave temples, the Jogeshwari Caves are dedicated to Lord Shiva and showcase remarkable Hindu architecture dating back to the 6th Century CE.

Learn More
