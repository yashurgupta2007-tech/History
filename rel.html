Unity In Diversity

Maharashtra has long been a meeting place of different faiths and cultures. From ancient Hindu temples and Buddhist monuments to Islamic shrines, Christian churches and Jewish synagogues, the state's religious heritage reflects centuries of peaceful coexistence, spirituality and architectural excellence.
