Forts of Maharashtra

Discover the magnificent forts that symbolize courage, military brilliance and the glorious legacy of the Maratha Empire.

The Legacy of Maharashtra's Forts

Maharashtra is home to more than 350 forts each standing as a testament of bravery, strategic genius and architectural excellence. These forts played a vital role in the rise of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and the Maratha Empire.

Types of Forts

Hill Forts

Built on mountains for strategic defense.

Sea Forts

Constructed along the coastline for naval protection.

Land Forts

Built on plains and important trade routes.
