Folk Songs Of Maharashtra

Discover the soulful music, devotional traditions and storytelling songs that form an important part of Maharashtra's culture.

The Musical Heritage of Maharashtra

Folk songs are an important part of Maharashtra's cultural heritage. They reflect the spiritual beliefs, daily life, traditions and emotions of its people.

From devotional Abhangs and Bhajans to traditional storytelling performances, these musical traditions have been passed down through generations.

Bharud

Bharud is a traditional folk performance combining music, singing, storytelling and acting to convey social and spiritual messages.

Learn More

Bhajan

Bhajans are devotional songs expressing devotion and spiritual beliefs through music and singing.

Learn More

Abhang

Abhangs are devotional songs traditionally associated with the Bhakti tradition and the spiritual heritage of Maharashtra.

Learn More

Palna Geet

Palna Geet are traditional Maharashtrian lullabies sung by mothers to soothe infants, expressing love, care and blessings of the child.

Learn More

Kirtan

Kirtan is a devotional storytelling tradition that combines music, singing, narration and spiritual teachings.

Learn More

Gavlan

Gavlan are devotional folk songs celebrating the playful childhood of Krishna, especially depicting the lives of cowherds and milkmaids.
