Preserving Maharashtra's Legacy

Museums and Art Galleries play a vital role in preserving Maharashtra's history, archaeology, traditional arts and cultural achievements. These institutions offer visitors a journey through the state's rich and diverse heritage.

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Vastu Sangrahalaya

Mumbai's premier museum housing thousands of archaeological, artistic and historical artefacts.

Learn More

Raja Dinkar Kelkar Museum

A unique museum in Pune displaying traditional Indian art, musical instruments and everyday artefacts.

Learn More

Dr. Bhau Daji Lad Museum

Mumbai's oldest museum showcasing the city's history, decorative arts and cultural heritage.

Learn More

Mani Bhavan Gandhi Museum

The historic residence associated with Mahatma Gandhi during India's freedom movement.

Learn More

Indian Institute Research in Numismatic Studies

India's first coin museum located in Nashik, dedicated to the history of Indian currency.

Learn More

RBI Monetary Museum

Learn about the evolution of India's monetary system through rare coins and currency exhibits.

Learn More

National Gallery of Modern Art

An important gallery exhibiting masterpieces of modern and contemporary Indian art.

Learn More

Tribal Museum

Showcasing the traditions, lifestyle and heritage of Maharashtra's tribal communities.

Learn More

Jahangir Art Gallery

It is India's most prestigious art galleries, showcasing contemporary and traditional artworks by renowned and emerging artists.
