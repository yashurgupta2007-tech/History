Traditional Musical Instruments

Discover the instruments that bring Maharashtra's folk traditions to life

The Instrumental Magic of Maharashtra

Music is an important part of Maharashtra's cultural heritage. Traditional instruments are used in folk dances, devotional performances, festivals, processions and celebrations, creating the distinctive sounds that continue to connect people with the state's rich cultural traditions.

Dholki

A small double-headed drum widely used in Lavani, folk performances, and traditional celebrations.

Learn More

Lezim

A handheld percussion instrument with metal jingles, commonly used during folk dances, processions, and cultural performances.

Learn More

Tutari

A curved traditional wind instrument historically used during ceremonies, celebrations, and important announcements.

Learn More

Pakhawaj

A barrel-shaped percussion instrument associated with devotional music and traditional Indian musical performances.

Ektara

A simple one-string instrument traditionally used by wandering singers and devotional performers.

Sambal

A traditional double-sided percussion instrument often played during folk dances, religious celebrations, and festive processions.
